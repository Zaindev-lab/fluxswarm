What is FluxSwarm?

Type a goal and a squad of 6 specialized agents — Planner, Architect, DevOps, TDD, Reviewer, Builder — plans, builds, tests, reviews and assembles it on a live task board you can watch. Generated files land in your project workspace, ready to use. Bring your own provider key for the strongest output — or a free OpenRouter key to trial free. Each launch is still a single credit. 1 credit per launch, credits never expire, no monthly fee.
